# Content Integrity — The One Day

## 1. Absolute Prohibition Against Fabrication

- **No Fabricated Credentials:** Never invent employment history, job titles, educational credentials, certifications, or technical project metrics. The resume provided by the owner is the sole authoritative source.
- **No Fabricated Personal Stories:** Never hallucinate personal anecdotes, travel experiences, artistic backgrounds, or fitness achievements.
- **No Stock Media Representation:** Do not substitute generic stock photos or AI-generated artwork and claim they represent the owner's personal work or life.

## 2. Handling Missing or Pending Information

- When resume data, photographs, or sketches are missing or pending, present structured placeholders clearly marked as pending (e.g., `// Pending asset upload: 2012 sketchbook scan`).
- Highlight gaps explicitly to the user rather than filling them with synthetic biographical text.

## 3. Copyright & Editorial Respect

- **Original Commentary:** The Reader section must feature original thoughts, personal reading context, and commentary. Do not embed copyrighted manga scans, pirated panels, or unlicensed anime broadcast captures.
- **Content Preservation:** Never overwrite, delete, or summarize away user-authored reflections or personal logs without explicit user consent.
