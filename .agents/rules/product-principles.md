# Product Principles — The One Day

## 1. Project Purpose & Positioning

- **Core Message:** “It was always going to be one day. Today is that day.” Beginning through small, consistent actions rather than waiting for ideal readiness.
- **Dual Identity:** Balance the credibility of a full-stack software engineer with genuine, creative exploration (sketching, calligraphy, origami, paper art, yoga, photography, reading).
- **Not a Template:** Avoid generic developer portfolio clichés (e.g., floating tech logos, generic hero slogans, dark-mode-only dashboard clones, gimmicky particle canvases).

## 2. Professional Discoverability

- Recruiters and technical peers must be able to discover engineering experience, skills, case studies, and contact details within seconds.
- Personal and creative sections enrich the owner's story, but must never obscure or distract from professional capabilities.

## 3. Progress Without Pressure

- Any journal, progress log, or action counter (e.g., The One Log) must emphasize returning to practice over consecutive streaks.
- Prohibit guilt-inducing indicators, missed-day penalties, or mandatory daily publishing pressure.

## 4. Authentic Scope & Lean MVP

- Prefer real, owner-supplied content over generic stock media or decorative placeholders.
- Maintain a lean MVP (homepage, engineer portfolio, about, contact, curated authentic creative material).
- Defer administrative backends, CMSs, databases, authentication, and microservices until a concrete requirement demands them.
