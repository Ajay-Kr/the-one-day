# The One Day — Project Context

**Last updated:** 2026-11-03  
**Phase:** Phase 0 — Discovery

## Project identity
- **Name:** The One Day
- **Established:** 2026
- **Type:** Personal portfolio and documented engineering/creative journey.
- **Core philosophy:** Stop waiting for the perfect moment; begin with one small action.
- **Core line:** “It was always going to be one day. Today is that day.”
- **Supporting line:** “One line. One pose. One photograph. One problem solved. One day at a time.”

## Project purpose
Create a distinctive, polished, publicly accessible portfolio that presents the owner as a full-stack software engineer with multiple interests. It should document building, learning, practising and exploring without falsely presenting the owner as an expert in every field.

## Owner background — current understanding
- Software Development Engineer / Full Stack Developer.
- Resume is still required and will be the authoritative source for employment, education, skills, projects and professional achievements.
- Creative interests: sketching, painting, calligraphy, origami and layered paper art.
- Has not practised some creative activities consistently for more than a decade; has a small number of old sketch photographs.
- Previously practised yoga and wants to rebuild gradually; has some photographs performing asanas.
- Enjoys personal photography; much of the existing collection is from the native place; has not travelled extensively.
- Reading and storytelling are longstanding interests; anime and manga are part of the personal reading history.

## Conceptual pillars
1. **The Engineer** — “I build things that work.” Resume, skills, projects, architecture and experiments.
2. **The Artist** — “One line at a time.” Sketching, painting, calligraphy, origami and paper art.
3. **The Practice** — “One movement at a time.” Yoga, asanas, strength, mobility and rebuilding practice.
4. **The Explorer** — “The world through my lens.” Places, people, small moments and personal photography.
5. **The Reader** — “Lost in another world.” Books, anime, manga, stories and personal reflections.
6. **The Journey** — “Still becoming.” Small attempts, progress logs and experiments.

This is a conceptual layout. It is not yet decided whether the site will be a single scrolling page, multi-page or hybrid. Sections need not have equal prominence.

## Important feature concept
**The One Log** may record small actions (a sketch, yoga pose, DSA problem, photograph, paper fold or technical concept). It should emphasise progress and returning to practice—not streaks, guilt or mandatory daily publishing. It may be a later enhancement rather than an MVP requirement.

## Audience and positioning
- Professional visitors/recruiters must quickly understand the owner's engineering identity and find projects, skills, experience and contact information.
- Other visitors should be able to explore the personal interests and journey.
- Personal sections should add depth without obscuring professional credibility.

## Design principles
- Editorial minimalism, creative journal, artistic portfolio and modern developer portfolio.
- Large typography, asymmetric layouts, subtle paper textures, annotations, personal photographs and restrained transitions.
- Avoid generic templates, excessive gradients/glassmorphism/parallax, overloaded animation and reduced readability.
- Authentic user-created content is preferred over generic stock imagery.
- Keep accessibility, responsiveness, performance and SEO in scope.

## Technical principles
- Treat this as a real product, but avoid premature complexity.
- Prefer modular architecture; avoid microservices without a real need.
- Do not introduce every technology from the learning wishlist.
- For every technology choice, document the problem, suitability, alternatives, trade-offs, learning value and whether it is MVP or experiment.
- The site must be deployable before advanced experiments are complete.

## Current provisional MVP
- Homepage and navigation.
- Professional portfolio.
- About and Contact.
- Curated personal/creative content using available authentic assets.
- Responsive design, accessibility, SEO, image optimisation and production deployment.
- Lightweight manually authored content initially; no database/CMS/admin/authentication unless discovery demonstrates a need.

## Pending user inputs
- Latest resume.
- Old sketches/artwork.
- Yoga/asana photographs.
- Personal photography.
- Optional context: approximate dates, places, stories and public-display restrictions.

## Known open decisions
- Single-page vs multi-page vs hybrid.
- Final visual direction and design system.
- Final information architecture and route structure.
- Framework/styling choices and hosting.
- Whether The One Log is MVP or post-launch.
- Whether a backend, database or CMS is justified.
